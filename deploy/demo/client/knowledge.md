# Quillmoor Cycle Works - knowledge base

Quillmoor Cycle Works is made up, and so is the town of Quillmoor.
Nothing here describes a real business, place or person.
Every price, time and address below is invented for the demo.

## QCW-01 Opening hours
Keywords: when, open, opening, hours, times, closed, today, tomorrow, weekend, saturday, sunday, monday, tuesday, wednesday, thursday, friday
The shop is open Tuesday to Friday from 8am to 6pm and on Saturdays from 9am to 4pm.
It is closed on Sundays and Mondays.

## QCW-02 Where the shop is
Keywords: where, address, located, location, find, entrance, directions, canal, bridge
The shop is at 12 Lantern Row in Quillmoor, beside the canal bridge.
The entrance is under the blue arch, and the workshop door is at the back of the yard.

## QCW-03 Getting here and parking
Keywords: bus, park, parking, car, travel, train, station, loading, rack
There are two short-stay loading bays in the yard for dropping off bikes.
The number 4 bus stops on Lantern Row, and Quillmoor station is a ten-minute ride along the canal path.

## QCW-04 What the shop does
Keywords: services, repair, repairs, fix, mend, rent, rental, parts, sell, offer, do you
Quillmoor Cycle Works repairs and services bikes, rents out bikes by the day or week, sells parts and refurbished bikes, and runs bike fittings and a free maintenance class.

## QCW-05 Servicing
Keywords: service, servicing, tune, tune-up, check, overhaul, full, basic
A basic service adjusts the brakes and gears, checks the tyres and cleans the chain, and is usually ready the same day if the bike arrives before 10am.
A full service also strips and cleans the drivetrain and replaces worn cables, and takes two working days.

## QCW-06 Punctures while you wait
Keywords: puncture, flat, tyre, tire, tube, wait, quick, inner
Punctures are repaired while you wait, usually in about twenty minutes, if a mechanic is free.

## QCW-07 E-bikes
Keywords: e-bike, ebike, electric, motor, battery, assist, pedelec
The workshop services e-bikes from most makers, including motor diagnostics.
Batteries are not opened or repaired in the shop; a faulty battery is sent back to its maker.

## QCW-08 How bookings work
Keywords: book, booking, appointment, slot, reserve, schedule, drop off
Repairs, services, rentals and fittings can be booked in this chat or at the counter.

## QCW-09 Changing a booking

## QCW-10 Walk-ins

## QCW-11 Collecting a bike

## QCW-25 Gears skipping or slipping
Keywords: gears, gear, skipping, slipping, jumping, changing, shifting, derailleur, clicking, indexing
Skipping gears are most often a slack cable.
With the bike in the gear it skips in, turn the barrel adjuster where the cable leaves the shifter half a turn anticlockwise and try again; repeat a little at a time.
If the chain or cassette is worn, adjusting will not help and a mechanic should look at it.

## QCW-26 Squeaking or rubbing brakes
Keywords: brake, brakes, squeak, squeaking, squeal, rub, rubbing, noise, noisy, pads
Squeaking rim brakes are usually dirty rims or pads: wipe the rims with a clean cloth and some soapy water and let them dry.
A disc brake that rubs often needs the wheel re-seated straight in its dropouts.
Brakes that feel weak or soft should be checked by a mechanic before the bike is ridden.

## QCW-27 Looking after a chain
Keywords: chain, oil, lube, lubricate, rusty, dry, clean, often, squeaky chain
Wipe the chain with a dry cloth every couple of weeks, put one drop of chain oil on each link, and wipe off what is left.
A chain that has rusted or stretched should be replaced.

## QCW-28 Tyre pressure and avoiding punctures
Keywords: tyre, tyres, tire, pressure, pump, inflate, psi, puncture, punctures, avoid, prevent
The right pressure is printed on the side of the tyre; check it every week, because tyres lose air slowly.
Tyres at the right pressure get far fewer punctures, and puncture-resistant tyres help on glass-strewn roads.

## QCW-29 E-bike battery care
Keywords: battery, batteries, charge, charging, winter, cold, store, storage, range, e-bike, ebike
Store an e-bike battery indoors, between about a third and two thirds charged, and away from frost.
Charge it at room temperature with its own charger.
