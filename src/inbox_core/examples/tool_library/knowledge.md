# Brambleford Tool Library - knowledge base

The Brambleford Tool Library is made up, and so is the town of Brambleford.
Nothing here describes a real organisation, place or person.

## TL-01 Opening hours
Keywords: when, open, opening, hours, times, closed, today, sunday, monday, tuesday, thursday, friday, horario, abierto, abren, cierran, hora, sábado, sabado, miércoles, miercoles
The library is open on Wednesdays from 5pm to 8pm and on Saturdays from 10am to 2pm.
It is closed on every other day.

## TL-02 Location
Keywords: where, address, located, find, entrance, dónde, donde, dirección, direccion, ubicación, ubicacion, encuentra
The library is in the old bus depot at 4 Mill Yard, behind the Brambleford market hall.
The entrance is the green door on the side of the building.

## TL-03 Parking and getting there
Keywords: bus, park, parking, car, bike, travel, aparcar, aparcamiento, coche, autobús, autobus, bicicleta, llegar
There are four short-stay parking spaces in Mill Yard for loading tools.
The number 7 bus stops at the market hall, two minutes' walk away, and there is a bike rack by the green door.

## TL-04 What a tool library is
Keywords: qué es, que es, biblioteca, herramientas, préstamo, prestamo
A tool library lends tools the way a book library lends books.
Members borrow a tool, use it at home, and bring it back, so the town shares one good drill instead of owning a hundred.

## TL-05 Who can join
Keywords: eligible, eligibility, live, work, age, 18, años, edad, mayor, inscribir, hacerme, socio, socia, hacerse socio, quién, quien, unirse, requisitos
Anyone aged 18 or over who lives or works in Brambleford can become a member.

## TL-06 How to join
Keywords: join, sign, register, member, membership, card, bring, hacerme, llevar, traer, hacerse socio, inscribirse, apuntarse, registrarse, carné, carne, documento
Bring a photo ID and something showing your address to the front desk during opening hours.
A volunteer sets up your membership card on the spot and you can borrow on the same day.

## TL-07 Loan period
Keywords: long, keep, loan, borrow, days, cuánto tiempo, cuanto tiempo, plazo, préstamo, prestamo, días, dias, semana
A loan lasts seven days.
Tools are collected and returned during opening hours.

## TL-08 Renewing a loan
Keywords: renovar, ampliar, prolongar, más tiempo, mas tiempo, extender
A loan can be renewed once for another seven days if nobody else has reserved the tool.
Ask a volunteer at the desk, or reply to your loan reminder.

## TL-09 Late returns
Keywords: tarde, retraso, devolver tarde, devolución, devolucion, multa
There are no late fines.
A volunteer sends a friendly reminder, and members with an overdue tool cannot borrow another until it is back.

## TL-10 How many tools at once
Keywords: many, once, same, limit, cuántas, cuantas, varias, límite, limite, a la vez, número, numero
Each member can borrow up to three tools at a time.

## TL-11 What tools there are
Keywords: qué herramientas, que herramientas, catálogo, catalogo, taladro, escalera, jardín, jardin
The library has about 400 tools: drills and drivers, sanders, saws, ladders, garden tools, a pressure washer, a carpet cleaner, sewing machines and a set of bike repair tools.

## TL-12 Online catalogue
Keywords: catálogo, catalogo, en línea, en linea, internet, web, buscar
The full list of tools, and whether each one is in or out, is in the online catalogue at catalogue.brambleford-tools.example.

## TL-13 Ladders
Keywords: escalera, escaleras, altura, subir
Ladders are lent for up to three days rather than seven, because they are in demand.
Members are shown how to check a ladder before each use when they collect it.

## TL-14 Power tool induction
Keywords: herramientas eléctricas, herramientas electricas, eléctrica, electrica, sierra, inducción, induccion, formación, formacion
Before borrowing a saw or a sander for the first time, members take a ten-minute safety induction with a volunteer at the desk.
Drills and drivers do not need an induction.

## TL-15 Protective gear
Keywords: protección, proteccion, gafas, guantes, seguridad, casco, mascarilla
Safety glasses, ear defenders, dust masks and gloves can be borrowed alongside any tool, and you can keep the dust masks.

## TL-16 Batteries and chargers
Keywords: batería, bateria, cargador, cargar, pilas
Cordless tools are lent with a charged battery and a charger.
Please return the charger with the tool.

## TL-17 Cleaning tools before returning them
Keywords: limpiar, limpieza, sucio, devolver limpio
Please bring tools back clean and dry.
Garden tools should be free of mud, and paint brushes and rollers should be washed out.

## TL-18 Returning when closed
Keywords: devolver, devolución, devolucion, buzón, buzon, cerrado
There is no drop box, so tools can only be returned during opening hours.
If you cannot make it, renew the loan instead of returning it late.

## TL-19 Someone else collecting for you
Keywords: recoger, otra persona, amigo, familiar, en mi nombre
Another adult can collect or return a tool for you if they bring your membership card.

## TL-20 Repair café
Keywords: reparar, reparación, reparacion, café, cafe, arreglar, taller
On the first Saturday of each month, volunteers run a repair café from 10am to 1pm where you can bring a broken household item and learn to fix it together.

## TL-21 Workshops
Keywords: taller, talleres, curso, aprender, clase
The library runs short evening workshops on basic skills such as putting up shelves, sharpening garden tools and simple bike repairs.
They are listed on the events page of the website.

## TL-22 Bike repair tools
Keywords: bicicleta, bici, pinchazo, rueda, reparar bici
The bike repair kit includes a work stand, tyre levers, a pump with gauges, a chain tool and cone spanners.
It is lent as one kit.

## TL-23 Accessibility
Keywords: accesibilidad, silla de ruedas, acceso, rampa, escalones
The green side door has step-free access and the counter has a lowered section.
Volunteers can carry heavy tools to your car in Mill Yard.

## TL-24 Languages
Keywords: idioma, idiomas, español, espanol, inglés, ingles, hablan
Volunteers at the desk speak English, and on Saturdays there is usually a Spanish-speaking volunteer as well.

## TL-25 Holidays
Keywords: festivo, festivos, vacaciones, navidad, cerrado
The library is closed for the last two weeks of December and on public holidays.

## TL-26 Lost membership card
Keywords: perdí, perdi, tarjeta, carné, carne, perdida, nueva tarjeta
If you lose your membership card, a volunteer can issue a new one at the desk when you show a photo ID.

## TL-27 Businesses
Keywords: empresa, negocio, profesional, comercial, autónomo, autonomo
The library lends to individuals for their own household projects.
It does not lend tools for paid or commercial work.

## TL-28 Volunteering
Keywords: voluntario, voluntaria, voluntariado, ayudar, colaborar
The library is run by volunteers.
To help on the desk, at the repair café or with tool maintenance, ask for a volunteer form at the desk.

## TL-29 Newsletter
Keywords: boletín, boletin, noticias, correo, suscribirse
There is a monthly email newsletter with new tools and upcoming workshops.
You can sign up on the website or ask at the desk.

## TL-30 Contact
Keywords: contacto, contactar, correo, email, teléfono, telefono
The library's inbox is desk@brambleford-tools.example.
Messages are read by volunteers, usually within a few days.
